The Single Dipper

Round single cold plunge. 1 to 2 people, ideally one at a time for full depth immersion.

Size
99cm internal diameter, 115cm external. 100.5cm total height, 100cm soaking depth.
Temperature
Cold only. The chiller unit cools to 3 to 5°C.
Seat
Internal seat approx 35cm wide, 30cm high.
Timber
Treated Thermowood, heat dried and thermally modified at 200°C, or Pinewood.
Interior
Stainless steel, or polypropylene in Blackout, Storm Grey, Frost Grey, Glacier Blue or Ice White.
Included
  • Dual stainless steel tightening bands
  • Drainage, internal base plug and external stainless steel side tap
  • Single stepper
  • Chiller unit installation, where a chiller is fitted
Options
Filtration system and chiller unit Handcrafted wooden lid with handles Canopy cover Large double stepper LED internal spotlight Air jet system Insulated floor base Timber stain
LED spotlight and air jet system are for polypropylene interiors.
Cold, or hot and cold

The Double Dipper

Oval two seater with two fixed seats, for residential or commercial use.

Size
Internal width approx 120cm, external 130cm. Internal length 190cm, external 200cm. Internal height 97cm, external 104cm.
Temperature
Cold with the chiller unit, 3 to 5°C. Hot and cold with the DualTemp unit, 3 to 5°C to 40°C. Or wood fired, with the integrated wood burning stove and chimney.
Seats
Seat height 28cm, width 40cm, backrest 66cm.
Water
800 litres. Dry weight 160kg.
Timber
Thermowood cladding, 22mm thick, heat dried and thermally modified at 200°C.
Interior
Fibreglass with two fixed seats, in Storm Grey, Glacier Blue, Ice White or Blackout.
Included
  • Curved Thermowood double step
  • Drainage, internal base plug and external stainless steel side tap
Options
Filtration system and chiller unit, cools to 3 to 5°C DualTemp unit, hot and cold, 3 to 5°C to 40°C Integrated wood burning stove with chimney Protective wooden housing for the chiller Full body foam insulation Underwater LED spotlight Air jet system Thermo insulated cover Timber stain Bottle holder Floating thermometer Surface skimmer
