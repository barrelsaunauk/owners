Connecting your sauna heater

For saunas with an electric heater. Pass this guide to your electrician before build day, so the supply is ready and planned around your garden.

What sits where

One heater, one supply

Your sauna needs a single dedicated supply for the heater. The heater has its control unit built in, so there is one connection point and nothing else to wire inside the hot room.

The heater

The pillar heater stands on the floor of the hot room, on your left as you walk in, behind its safety rail. The supply connects at the heater position.

The control panel

The digital wall panel sets the temperature and timer. It connects to the heater by its own control cable, so it is not a second mains supply.

App control

Where WiFi control is fitted, the sauna can be switched on and set from your phone. It runs from the same panel and needs no extra wiring.

Planning the cable route

From your board to the sauna, out of sight

  1. 1
    One cable from your board
    A single dedicated supply runs from your consumer unit to the sauna. Your electrician supplies and sizes it for the length of the run.
  2. 2
    Routed round, not across
    Armoured cable clipped along a wall or fence, or through an existing duct, so a finished patio or new lawn stays untouched.
  3. 3
    An isolator at the sauna
    A weatherproof switch on the outside wall on the heater side, then the supply runs through the wall to the heater.
For your electrician

Connection specification

Figures below are for a 9kW pillar heater with digital panel control. The heater's own installation manual and rating plate always take priority.

ConnectionHardwired. A sauna heater is never connected by a plug.
CircuitA dedicated circuit from the consumer unit, separate from any hot tub or garden supply.
Single phase230V 1N. Supply cable 3 x 10mm², drawing around 39A at full power.
Three phase400V 3N. Supply cable 5 x 2.5mm².
ProtectionBreaker and RCD protection sized by your electrician to the heater manual and BS 7671.
CableArmoured cable, run along a wall or fence where possible. Inside the hot room, cable must be rated for sauna temperatures.
IsolatorWeatherproof isolator outside the sauna, on the heater side.
LightingThe heater's control unit can also switch the sauna light, up to 100W.
StandardsAll work to BS 7671 and Part P of the Building Regulations, by a registered electrician (NICEIC, NAPIT, ECA or equivalent).

Before first use

A quick checklist

  • Make sure the heater stones are loaded as shown in the heater manual before switching on.
  • Check nothing is resting on or against the heater or its safety rail.
  • Test the RCD using its test button.
  • Switch on at the panel and set your temperature. The first heat can give off a slight smell as the heater beds in. This is normal.
  • Keep the door closed and the vents open while the sauna heats.
