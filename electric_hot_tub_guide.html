Connecting your electric hot tub

For Smart Electric and heat pump builds. Pass this guide to your electrician before your tub arrives, so the supply is planned and your garden stays exactly as you want it.

What sits where

Everything electrical is in one place

Your tub is built so there is a single connection point. Nothing needs wiring around the outside of the tub, and no cable has to cross a patio or lawn.

The equipment bay

The heater, the jet pump and the Balboa control box sit together inside the cladding, behind one service door on one side of the tub. This is where the supply connects.

The heat pump

Where a heat pump is fitted, it is a separate unit that stands on the ground beside the tub on the service side, connected to the tub by its water pipes.

Your choice of side

When we position your tub we turn it so the service door and heat pump face whichever side is closest to your supply. Just let us know which side suits you.

Circle and square electric tubs

Balboa heater and control system, jets, LED lighting and the optional 7kW heat pump.

Planning the cable route

From your board to the tub, out of sight

  1. 1
    One cable from your board
    A single dedicated supply runs from your consumer unit to the tub. Your electrician supplies and sizes it for the length of the run.
  2. 2
    Routed round, not across
    Buried armoured cable or a duct can follow the edge of the garden or a wall, so a finished patio or new lawn stays untouched.
  3. 3
    An isolator beside the tub
    A weatherproof switch within sight of the tub, then a short tail into the equipment bay from underneath.
For your electrician

Connection specification

Hand this table to your electrician. The exact ratings for your tub are on the data plate inside the service door, and those always take priority.

ConnectionHardwired. Electric and heat pump tubs are not fitted with a plug, as the load is too high for a 13A socket.
CircuitA dedicated circuit from the consumer unit, with nothing else connected to it.
Supply ratingTaken from the data plate inside the service door. Your electrician sizes the circuit so the heater, jets and lights can all run together.
Protection30mA RCD, Type A, with a curve C MCB to handle pump start up.
CableSized by your electrician for the route length and installation method. Armoured cable for buried runs, flexible H07RN-F for the final tail into the equipment bay.
IsolatorWeatherproof rotary isolator, IP54 or better, within sight of the tub, 1.5 to 3m away and outside zones 0 and 1.
Heat pumpAllow for the heat pump's power at the tub. Its own data plate confirms its rating and connection.
EarthingCorrect earthing with supplementary bonding to any accessible metalwork around the tub.
StandardsAll work to BS 7671 and Part P of the Building Regulations, by a registered electrician (NICEIC, NAPIT, ECA or equivalent).

Before first switch on

A quick checklist

  • Fill the tub so the water sits above every jet and intake. A pump must never run dry.
  • Check all pipework and unions for leaks.
  • Test the RCD using its test button.
  • Switch on. The control system runs a priming sequence for a few minutes before it heats. This is normal.
  • Set your temperature on the panel and briefly test the jets and lights.
